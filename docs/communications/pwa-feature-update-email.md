# Bible Compass feature-update email

Draft for review. Not sent. This version describes the upcoming release; use the
production link and confirm release status before sending.

**Subject:** Coming soon to Bible Compass: your daily verse

**Preview text:** Save Bible Compass to your Home Screen and choose a time for a daily verse.

Hi,

We’re preparing a small update to help you make room for Scripture in your day.

With the next Bible Compass release, you’ll be able to:

- **Save Bible Compass to your Home Screen** for quick access with its own app icon.
- **Choose a daily verse reminder** and set the time and timezone that work for you.
- **Explore further** by tapping a notification to open the verse and continue reading.

Reminders are optional. Turn them on or off in **Your Profile**, where you can
also adjust the timing. No account is needed.

On iPhone, open Bible Compass in Safari, choose **Share → Add to Home Screen**,
then launch it from the saved icon. When the update is live, enable reminders in
Your Profile and allow notifications when asked. Check your Banners and Focus
settings if you want a banner alert. Delivery may be delayed by your device or
the notification service.

**Open Bible Compass:** https://bible-compass-three.vercel.app

No streaks to keep. No pressure to catch up. Just an invitation to pause and
spend a moment in Scripture.

The Bible Compass team
